Fairfax Water BILLS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull fairfaxwater diagnose            paperpull fairfaxwater diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull fairfaxwater pilot               paperpull fairfaxwater pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull fairfaxwater all                 paperpull fairfaxwater all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull fairfaxwater resume              paperpull fairfaxwater resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull fairfaxwater verify              paperpull fairfaxwater verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull fairfaxwater diagnose  ->  paperpull fairfaxwater pilot  ->  paperpull fairfaxwater all
Sign in yourself in the window login.bat opens, and keep it OPEN.

READ-ONLY. It downloads water bills only. It NEVER pays a bill, sets
up autopay, stores a card or bank account, starts or stops service, or
changes paperless or contact settings.

Scope is the quarterly bills that have a PDF, and Fairfax Water keeps
those for the last year only, so run it every few months to keep them
all.

Your files are in this folder, under Statements\.
Spouse's:  "Fairfax Water Statements - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
