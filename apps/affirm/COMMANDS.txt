Affirm LOAN AGREEMENTS  -  commands   (READ-ONLY)
============================================================
Run these from the PaperPull folder (paperpull.bat on Windows,
./paperpull on macOS). login.bat is the one double-click that stays.

  Task            You                                 Spouse
  --------------  ----------------------------------  ----------------------------------
  Sign in         login.bat                           login.bat spouse
  Look (no d/l)   paperpull affirm diagnose            paperpull affirm diagnose --account spouse
  Pilot (test)    paperpull affirm pilot               paperpull affirm pilot --account spouse
  Full run        paperpull affirm all                 paperpull affirm all --account spouse
  Continue        paperpull affirm resume              paperpull affirm resume --account spouse
  Re-check PDFs   paperpull affirm verify              paperpull affirm verify --account spouse

FLOW:  login.bat  ->  paperpull affirm diagnose  ->  paperpull affirm pilot  ->  paperpull affirm all
Sign in yourself in the window login.bat opens, with your phone number
and the text-message code, and keep it OPEN.

READ-ONLY. It downloads loan agreements only, one per loan. It NEVER
makes a payment, sets up autopay, stores a card or bank account, takes
a new loan, or changes contact details.

Scope is each loan's agreement, the Truth in Lending disclosure under
Loan terms, settled loans included. A pay-over-time account has no
monthly statement.

Your files are in this folder, under Loan Agreements\.
Spouse's:  "Affirm Statements - spouse".
Full command reference: COMMANDS.txt in the parent folder.
